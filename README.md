## Featured research

### [Physics-Informed Robot Condition Monitoring — KUKA KR16 L6](https://github.com/Mo22Ha/kuka-research-private)

**Can combining physical robot dynamics with statistical time-series models produce a reliable Healthy-state reference and improve condition monitoring compared with purely data-driven methods?**

My investigation connects Newtonian and Euler–Lagrange mechanics, numerical modeling, motor-current proxies, sensor geometry, video-based kinematics, autoregressive models and experimental comparisons.

The [public research archive](https://github.com/Mo22Ha/kuka-research-private) contains reports, LaTeX, figures and selected numerical results. Current findings expose limits in physical identifiability, predictive improvement and alarm calibration across recording days. They do not establish a validated industrial monitoring system.

## Research interests and next directions

- **Applied mathematics:** dynamical systems, inverse problems, identifiability and numerical methods.
- **Computational physics:** rigid-body dynamics and physical observation models.
- **Statistical inference:** time series, Bayesian modeling, uncertainty quantification and validation under changing conditions.
- **Philosophy of science:** explanation, idealization and the distinction between predictive agreement and evidence for a mechanism.

Bayesian uncertainty quantification and the philosophical analysis are proposed directions, not completed KUKA findings.
